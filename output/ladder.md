## Key isolations

**R2h − R2 = +0.005566 — sign does NOT flip.** Restricting the stacked control
pool from 741 → 41 (the exact pool CS uses), changing nothing else, moves the
interacted-FE stacked estimate only from −0.09892 to −0.09335 — well within the
SE (0.036). R2h is negative **and** significant (t = −2.57). No cohorts were lost
for want of controls (all 11 retained). Flag: the 741-vs-41 control pool is **not**
what separates the stacked estimate from CS; harmonizing the pool leaves the
stacked estimate essentially unchanged and still supporting P&P.

**R5d (diagnostic) = +0.03028 vs R5 = +0.04477; diff = −0.01449.** Forcing CS to
admit the 700 always-`no.req==1` non-changers as never-treated controls (a
treatment history we believe false) pulls CS down by only 0.0145 and it **stays
positive**. Decomposition device only — not a candidate specification.

**R5b — aggregation isolation (both flags computed):**
- R5b on R2 atoms (741 pool) = **−0.06114**
- R5b on R2h atoms (41 pool) = **−0.06148**
- CS pooled target R5 = +0.04477

Both versions move from their stacked baselines (R2 −0.099, R2h −0.093) toward R5
by ≈ +0.037, and land at essentially the same value (−0.061) regardless of pool.
**Flag triggered: AGGREGATION-driven** (both move), **not** pool-driven. Applying
CS's cross-cohort weights to the stacked cohort atoms closes part of the R2↔R5 gap,
but the atoms stay negative, so a residual gap to CS's +0.045 remains — that
residual is the estimand/atoms, not the control pool.

**R6 — disaggregated CS on the 41 pool:** 13 cohort ATT(g); **53.8% negative**,
**38.5% individually significant at 5%**. Strongly heterogeneous (e.g. 2004
−0.260 sig, 2018 −0.318-equivalent; 2017 +0.722 sig, 2013 +0.302 sig, 2002 +0.222
sig). Full ATT(g) and ATT(e) with CIs in `atoms_harmonized.csv`.
